# Main_Project
Virtual Engagement Project – Gen Z Smartphone Brand Switching


## Objective
To understand how rising smartphone prices drive brand switching and
refurbished phone adoption among Gen Z in India.

## Summary of Expected Insights (Hypotheses)
- Rising prices of new flagship and mid-range phones are expected to make
  Gen Z more price-sensitive and less loyal to a single brand.
- Gen Z buyers are likely to switch toward brands offering better
  specs-for-price, especially in the mid-range segment.
- Refurbished phones are expected to be considered mainly by
  budget-conscious buyers who want premium brands at lower prices.
- Trust factors (warranty, battery health, seller reliability) are expected
  to be the main barrier to refurbished adoption.

*These are hypotheses to be validated through survey data.*

## Key Learning
Question design and clean survey responses decide how reliable
consumer-behaviour conclusions are.

## Tools
Excel, Power BI, Survey Research

## Data Source
Smartprix (smartphone model and price details)
